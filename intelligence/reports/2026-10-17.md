# GrowthSpace Saudi workforce intelligence — 17 October 2026

Reviewed 17 October 2026, Saudi time. This is an additional previously missed announcement published **12 October 2026**, not a newly issued policy today.

## Sector skills frameworks and training targets
**Source:** Saudi Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development, 12 October 2026.
**Official source:** https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/media-center/news/الراجحي-يشارك-في-منتدى-الرياض-الاقتصادي

**What changed:** At the Riyadh Economic Forum, the ministry outlined 16 sector skills councils, 13 sector skills frameworks and a Saudi skills classification guide with over 10,000 skills covering over 2,000 occupations. Its Wa'ad campaign aims to offer over four million training opportunities across two phases. These are a description of existing structures and a target, not four million newly completed training places.

**Why it matters to GrowthSpace:** Employer training procurement may increasingly favor role-specific capabilities aligned with Saudi sector frameworks.

## Three practical implications
1. Map GrowthSpace programs to relevant Saudi sector skills frameworks.
2. Offer skills-gap assessments and measurable learning outcomes.
3. Monitor ministry partnership and program delivery opportunities.

No other significant newly published announcement was verified in this review. The 16 October briefing remains available separately.
