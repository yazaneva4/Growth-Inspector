## 1. Corporate remote-working arrangements (15 October)
**Source:** Reuters reporting republished by Dawn, 15 October 2026: https://www.dawn.com/news/2037376/attacks-in-saudi-arabia-prompt-finance-firms-to-let-staff-work-from-home-sources-say

## 2. FII hybrid attendance plans and Misk Global Forum cancellation (13 October)

**Source:** SAP MENA, 4 October 2026: https://news.sap.com/mena/2026/10/business-ai-takes-center-stage-as-saudi-organizations-advancehr-transformation-at-sap-hr-connect-ksa-2026/

These are survey responses, not a measure of all Saudi employers.

**Why GrowthSpace should care:** Evidence supports testing practical AI leadership and workforce transformation offerings, with outcomes tied to business value.
